ABOUT THE COMPANY

Practical engineering support, grounded in field work.

Established in July 2010 under the proprietorship of Mr. P. Robert, RJ Engineering moved to its corporate office in Aralvaimozhi in June 2014. The company undertakes field work for wind energy and electrical infrastructure projects.

Project execution is supported by workshop facilities that include lathe, milling and drilling equipment, a dedicated material storage area, and transport vehicles for moving people, tools and materials to work sites.
